Who We Are

We built Right Chews because we couldn't find what we were looking for.

Every protein bar we tried was either packed with artificial flavors and fillers, or tasted like cardboard trying to be a dessert. We knew there had to be a better way — real ingredients, real flavor, real macros.

Right Chews was born in Orlando, FL, out of a simple belief: if you're disciplined enough to count your protein, you deserve a snack that actually tastes good. We spent months perfecting three flavors — Chocolate, Red Velvet, and Blondie — until each one hit the mark.

Today we're on a mission to get Right Chews into gyms, supplement shops, and homes across the country. Every brownie is 20g of protein, clean label, and made to the same standard we'd hold any product we eat ourselves.
